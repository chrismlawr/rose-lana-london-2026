Fri 6 – Mon 9 November 2026

Rose & Lana's London Weekend

Shoreditch shopping, an Islington Saturday, a lazy Sunday pick and a cosy night in. Plan first, then every spot with its Google Maps link, then where to eat and drink.

The plan

Times are loose. Warnings in coral are opening-hour clashes worth knowing.

Friday
6 Nov · Spitalfields & Shoreditch
  1. Midday
    Arrive, drop bags and chill
  2. 4:00pm
    Coffee and cake at Townhouse5 Fournier St, a 1700s house with a café hidden downstairs⚑ Closes 6pm
  3. By 6pm
    Nobody's Child in Spitalfields MarketCloses 6pm, so go first
  4. By 7pm
    Shop around: InSpitalfields, The Mercantile, Goodhood, Atika, Rokit, Rough Trade, Earl of East, ReformationSpitalfields first, then Hanbury St and Brick Lane, ending on Redchurch St⚑ Rough Trade East closes at 6pm on Fridays, so do it before 6
  5. 7:15pm
    Dinner at Lahpet (Burmese)Bethnal Green Rd, 5 min walk from Redchurch St
  6. 9:15pm
    Drinks at Seed LibraryCocktails, vinyl DJ from 9pm. Kay joins for drinks
Saturday
7 Nov · Islington, then back east
  1. 11:00am
    5K run around Highbury and Islington
  2. After
    Coffee and bites around Camden Passage: Pophams, Uchi Bake, Katsute 100 and Japanese Curry Bread⚑ Pophams and the curry bread both close at 4pm, and Pophams pastries sell out early
  3. Afternoon
    Camden Passage antique market, Upper Street and AnthropologieSaturday is one of the two big market days
  4. 3:00pm
    Home to rest, shower and get ready
  5. 5:30pm
    Drinks at Homeboy (Essex Rd)
  6. 7:45pm
    Dinner at Som Saa (Thai)Commercial St, E1. About 25 min from Homeboy, so leave by 7:15
  7. ~10pm
    Blues Kitchen ShoreditchLive band then DJs, open till 3am. 10 min walk from Som Saa
Sunday
8 Nov · slow start, pick one
  1. Midday
    Slow start, then choose:
    Option A · Classic Central
    Covent Garden, Seven Dials, Carnaby, Oxford St and Harry Potter at House of MinaLima (Soho), roast at Blacklock Soho. Book Blacklock.
    Option B · Marylebone
    Farmers' market (10am–2pm, so aim to arrive by 1), bakeries, Marylebone High St, cosy pub roast
    Option C · Hampstead
    Crêpes at La Crêperie de Hampstead, Hampstead Heath and Parliament Hill, village wander, roast at The Holly Bush
  2. Evening
    Cosy night in, movies and chill
Monday
9 Nov · home time
  1. Early
    Breakfast at a café nearbyVertige or La Maison open from 7–8am on Mondays
  2. Morning
    Jess starts her prep, girls pack up
  3. ~10:30am
    Head to the airportLana's flight is at 1:50pm

Every stop in the plan, with Google rating, hours on the day and a Maps link.

Friday

Townhouse

Café · gallery shop
4.5 ★★★★★182 Google reviews

A gallery of ceramics, antiques and books in an 18th-century house, with a tiny café in the old basement kitchen and a glass-roofed courtyard.

What reviewers sayPeople call it a hidden gem that feels like sitting in someone's period kitchen. The cardamom cake and lattes get repeat praise, and many first-timers nearly miss the café downstairs.
📍 5 Fournier St, E1🕒 Fri 11am–6pm · closed Mon→ Head straight downstairs for the café.
